Grasmere

Grasmere is a quiet place on your Mac for your best ideas. It’s made for writers, students and anyone learning something: write documents, link them together, and come back to find how they connect. It looks after your words, keeps out of your way, and never asks you to hand your documents over to anyone.

Get Grasmere on the Mac App Store: a free 7-day trial, then a single purchase. It needs macOS 26 or later.

What you can do

Write

  • Plain Markdown files in a folder you choose; no database, no custom syntax.
  • Markdown styled in place as you type, with the marks kept faint.
  • Headings, lists, task boxes, quotes, tables, footnotes and front matter.
  • Tables drawn as tables; Tab moves between cells.
  • Pictures shown under their links; paste or drop one to save and link it.
  • Diagrams written in Mermaid, drawn beneath the block. Coming soon
  • Code blocks coloured by language. Coming soon
  • Fold sections under their headings.
  • Autosave, recovery after a crash, and spelling as you type.

Link

  • Ordinary Markdown links between documents, to a heading or to a passage.
  • Links rewritten when you rename or move a document, in Grasmere or in Finder, with Undo.
  • A link to a document that doesn't exist yet makes it when you follow it.
  • Previews when you rest the pointer on a link.
  • Linked From, Links To and unlinked mentions in the margin, with one-click linking.
  • Broken links listed for the whole project.
  • Convert [[wiki-links]] to Markdown links.

Find your way

  • The contents: folders in your own order, set by dragging.
  • Open a document by name (⌘O), and search every document.
  • The Index: the project as a deck of linked cards.
  • Split view, and a document open on its own.
  • Back and forward through where you've been.
  • Reopens at the document you were on.

Plan and track

  • Tasks with due dates, across every document, in the margin's Tasks list.
  • A journal: today's entry with ⌘T, the days before and after.
  • Word targets with a deadline, and a running count of today's words.
  • Front matter as editable fields in the margin. Coming soon
  • An optional Markdown check that lists problems with their lines. Coming soon

Capture

  • Quick capture from any app (⌃⌥⌘N) or the menu bar, into today's entry.
  • Share from other apps into a project.
  • Save a link or text you've just copied, from a card that offers it.

Ask (optional, on your Mac)

  • Ask Your Documents: answers from your own documents, with citations.
  • A summary of the project.
  • New Document with Questions: questions to write from, for an essay, argument, story scene, runbook and more.
  • A follow-up question about the passage you're on.
  • Apple's on-device model by default; Claude or ChatGPT with your own key if you choose.

Publish and export

  • A folder of chapters as one PDF: a manuscript or a typeset book.
  • A document as PDF or Word.
  • A website from a project.
  • Anki flashcards.

Look and feel

  • Paper for the day; Slate, Graphite and Soot for the evening.
  • Focus mode, which fades all but the paragraph you're writing.
  • Follows your Mac's text size, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion.

Keep safe

  • History of each document in the margin, from git.
  • Optional auto-commit to git.
  • Writing Replay: a timestamped record of how a document was written. Experimental

Automate

  • Shortcuts actions to open, make, append to, search, check, export and publish documents, which also run from Terminal. Coming soon
  • Markdown files previewed in Finder with Quick Look.

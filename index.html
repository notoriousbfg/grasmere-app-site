Send.

Export a Word file from the Review screen. Your open comments go with it.

Step 2

They review.

In Word, Pages or Google Docs, with comments and tracked changes as usual. They don’t need Grasmere.

Step 3

Import.

Drop the file back in. Every comment and change appears beside your text, ready to accept, reject or reply to. Changes you made in the meantime are kept.

Grasmere

Grasmere is a quiet place on your Mac for your best ideas. It’s made for writers, students and anyone learning something: write documents, link them together, and come back to find how they connect. It looks after your words, keeps out of your way, and never asks you to hand your documents over to anyone.

Get Grasmere on the Mac App Store: a free 7-day trial, then a single purchase. It needs macOS 26 or later.

What you can do

  • Write in plain, standard Markdown files, in any folder you choose.
  • Link documents with ordinary Markdown links that update when you rename or move a file, even in Finder.
  • See headings, lists and links styled as you type, with the Markdown marks kept faint and in place.
  • Export a document as a PDF or a Word file, or publish a folder as a manuscript, a book or a website.
  • Keep a journal, track tasks with due dates, and capture a thought from any app.
  • Write in focus mode, with themes for day and evening.
  • Draw Mermaid diagrams and colour code blocks, all on your Mac. Coming soon
  • If you want it, have Apple’s on-device model answer from your documents, or ask you questions to write from.

No account, no tracking, and nothing leaves your Mac unless you send it. More on why Grasmere is made this way.

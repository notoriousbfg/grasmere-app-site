What will you write next?

Grasmere is a new type of Markdown editor for writers and researchers, with sophisticated features to take you from an idea to a manuscript or thesis.

Download for macOS 1.2 · macOS 26+
01 Plan Make a plan for your project. Collect your research from websites, books and papers so you can cite it later. Bring together all your tasks in one list. Web Clipping Research Tasks Index Cards Journal
02 Draft A pure Markdown writing experience, free from distraction. Find interesting links between your documents and brainstorm ideas with the on-device model. Writing Links Citations Focus Mode Writing Goals Brainstorm
03 Review Share your documents with colleagues & peers and extract their feedback. Finalise your sequence as you prepare to publish. Review Word Round Trip The Margin Running Order
04 Publish Publish your manuscript in PDF or Word Doc, complete with citations and formatting. Publish your project as a website with one click. Manuscript PDF Word Website

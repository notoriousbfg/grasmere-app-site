What’s New

Each version of Grasmere, and what it added. The guides describe the current version; where a feature arrived after 1.0, its section says New in 1.1 (and so on). Your version is under Grasmere ▸ About Grasmere in the app.

1.1 · coming soon

  • Convert Wiki-Links. Project ▸ Convert Wiki-Links rewrites every [[wiki-link]] in a project as a standard Markdown link, for notes brought from Obsidian: aliases, headings and embedded pictures are kept, links to notes that aren’t there are left as written, and the whole conversion can be undone. See Links and Backlinks and Support.
  • Writing Replay (experimental). A record of how a note is written, played back and exportable as a web page, with trusted timestamps. Off until turned on in Settings ▸ General. See Writing Replay.
  • Standard buttons on the sheets, in the theme’s colours, and no trailing ellipses on menu items.

1.0 · October 2026

The first release: notes as plain Markdown files, links that stay correct when notes are renamed or moved, the margin of backlinks and mentions, the Index, tasks and due dates, daily notes and quick capture, word targets, focus mode and split view, publishing to PDF, a website or Anki, and Ask Your Notes with Apple’s on-device model. A free 7-day trial, then a single purchase.

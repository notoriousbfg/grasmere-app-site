Margaret

Has had plot 13, next to mine, for thirty years, and grows the best leeks on the site. She’s taught me more than any book.

Her rules, more or less in her words:

  • Plant marigolds between the tomatoes: the smell keeps the whitefly away.
  • Never leave soil bare over winter. Sow a green manure, or cover it.
  • Water in the evening, at the roots, not on the leaves.

We swap seeds every spring. Her runner beans are a variety her father grew, and she won’t tell me where he got them.

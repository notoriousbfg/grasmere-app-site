Why beans come before brassicas

Peas and beans carry bacteria in small nodules on their roots that take nitrogen from the air and turn it into a form plants can use. When the plants are cut down and their roots left in the soil, some of that nitrogen stays behind for the next crop. Brassicas are leafy and need plenty of nitrogen, so they follow the beans.

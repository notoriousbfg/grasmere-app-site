Examples

A small sample notebook to practise on: a first year on an allotment. A short journal follows the autumn jobs, a few notes explain crop rotation, companion planting and compost, and Margaret, who has the next plot, gives most of the good advice. Start Here uses these notes to show what Grasmere does.

  1. Jobs for October: tasks with due dates
  2. Sowing Calendar: a table
  3. The Allotment: a picture, and headings to link to
  4. Margaret: a note other notes link to, and quote
  5. Ideas: a folder in an order of its own
  6. The journal: two daily notes
  7. Why beans come before brassicas: an insight saved from an AI conversation

This list also sets the order of the examples folder in the contents. Drag a note there and the list changes to match.

Crop Rotation

Growing each family of vegetables in a different bed each year, always in the same order. Pests and diseases that build up in the soil lose their host, and each crop finds the ground the way it likes it.

The usual order on four beds:

  1. Beans and peas, which fix nitrogen from the air
  2. Brassicas (cabbages, kale, broccoli), which are hungry for that nitrogen
  3. Roots (carrots, parsnips, beetroot), which fork in freshly manured soil
  4. Potatoes, which break the ground up for the beans

The plan is drawn on The Allotment. Compost goes on before the potatoes and the brassicas.

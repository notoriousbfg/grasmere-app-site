Compost

Two bays made of old pallets behind the shed: one filling, one rotting. Greens (peelings, grass cuttings, spent plants) and browns (cardboard, straw, dry leaves) in roughly equal parts, turned once a month.

It takes about a year. When a bay is ready it goes on the potato bed in autumn, as the rotation says.

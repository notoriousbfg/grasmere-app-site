Companion Planting

Growing plants side by side because one helps the other: by confusing pests, drawing in pollinators, or giving shade.

The one I’m sure of, from Margaret: marigolds between the tomatoes. The link points at that very line in her note. In Grasmere, a small number beside the line there shows that this note quotes it.

Others to try next year:

  • Nasturtiums beside the beans, as a sacrifice for the blackfly
  • Carrots between rows of onions or leeks, whose smell hides them from carrot fly

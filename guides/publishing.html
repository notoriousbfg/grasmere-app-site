Publishing

Your documents are plain files, so they already work anywhere. When you want something more finished, Grasmere can typeset them as a PDF, build a website, or make flash cards.

Publish a folder to PDF

Right-click a folder in the contents and choose Publish to PDF, or use the File menu: it reads Publish Project to PDF, or Publish “chapters” to PDF while a folder is highlighted in the contents with the arrow keys. Grasmere typesets all its documents as one PDF, in the folder’s order: each document becomes a chapter on a new page, titled by its first heading, and each folder inside becomes a part.

Give the PDF a title and, if you like, an author, then choose a style:

  • Manuscript: standard manuscript format, as publishers and agents ask for. Double-spaced 12 pt Times, one-inch margins, a title page with the word count, and the author, title and page number at the top of each page.
  • Book: your theme’s typography, large chapter titles, a divider page for each part, and page numbers at the foot.

The order comes from the folder’s index.md; see The Contents. When the PDF is saved, Show in Finder takes you to it.

To put a long piece in order and read it through before you publish it, open the folder as a manuscript: see The Manuscript View. Its Export button publishes the same PDF, or the whole folder as one Word document.

Export a document as a PDF

File ▸ Export as PDF (⌘P) typesets the open document on A4 or Letter pages.

Export a document as a Word document

New in 1.1. File ▸ Export as Word Document (⇧⌘E) saves the open document as a .docx, for handing in, or for someone who edits in Word. It’s a proper Word document, not a picture of one: the headings are Word’s Heading 1, 2 and 3 (so they fill the navigation pane and a table of contents), the lists, tables, quotations and code are Word’s own styles, pictures are embedded, and web links are clickable. Change the style set in Word and the whole document follows. Links to other documents are shown as their text; a link to a heading in the same document jumps to it. The pages are A4 or Letter, with page numbers at the foot.

Build a website

Project ▸ Build Website (⌥⌘B) turns your project into a website, in a _site folder inside it: a page for each document, with links that work, the pictures in them, and a list of the documents that link to each page.

  • The home page shows the project’s name, the Summary section of project.md, and the contents.
  • Each page is styled with your light and dark themes. There’s no JavaScript and nothing that tracks visitors.
  • A document with draft: true or publish: false in its front matter is left out, and links to it become plain text.
  • Linked files (such as PDFs), saved web pages (the research folder) and saved insights are often other people’s work, so they’re left out unless you include them in Settings ▸ Automation.
  • Grasmere shows you what will be published before the first build. After that, rebuilding only changes what has changed, and it never touches files it didn’t write.

When it’s done, Open in Browser shows you the site.

Publish

Project ▸ Publish (⇧⌘P) saves your documents, builds the website if you’ve asked it to, commits anything waiting to git, then runs a Shortcut of your choosing with the project folder: to upload the site, say, or to push it. Choose the Shortcut in Settings ▸ Automation ▸ Publish with.

Export to Anki

Grasmere can turn documents into flash cards for Anki, with the document’s title on the front and the document on the back.

  • File ▸ Export as Anki Deck exports the open document.
  • Right-click a folder and choose Export Folder to Anki for every document in it.
  • Pick several documents in the contents, right-click, and choose Export Documents to Anki.

Importing a later export into Anki updates the cards and keeps your reviews.

Ask Your Notes and AI

Grasmere has a few optional tools that use a language model to work with your notes. They’re off to one side: you can ignore them entirely, and nothing is sent anywhere until you ask.

Ask Your Notes (⇧⌘A)

Project ▸ Ask Your Notes… (⇧⌘A) opens the Ask tab in the margin. Type a question, and the answer is written from your notes alone, with numbered citations. Click a citation to open the note at the passage it came from.

  • Copy copies the answer, with its citations as links.
  • Save as Insight saves it as a note in your insights folder, titled with the question, with the citations as links and confidence: unverified until you’ve checked it.
  • Questions you’ve asked are listed under Asked earlier until you close the window. They aren’t saved.

Generate Summary

Project ▸ Generate Summary writes a short overview of the project into the Summary section of project.md, which is shown at the top of the contents. The rest of project.md is left as it is, and you can edit the summary like any other text.

Copy as Context (⌥⌘C)

Edit ▸ Copy as Context (⌥⌘C), or its button in the running head, copies the open note together with the titles and openings of every note it links to and from, ready to paste into a conversation with Claude, ChatGPT or any other assistant. Grasmere itself sends nothing anywhere. Settings ▸ AI ▸ Copy as Context sets how many words it copies (4,000 by default).

Which model answers

Settings ▸ AI ▸ Provider chooses the model for Ask Your Notes and Generate Summary:

  • On-device (Apple Intelligence), the default. It runs privately on your Mac, is free, and works offline. It needs Apple Intelligence to be turned on.
  • Claude (Anthropic) or ChatGPT (OpenAI), with your own API key from that company. You can choose the model too.

Your API keys are stored in your Mac’s keychain.

What is sent, and when

With the on-device model, nothing leaves your Mac.

With Claude or ChatGPT:

  • When you ask a question, your question and excerpts from the notes Grasmere picks as relevant are sent to that company to answer it.
  • When you generate a summary, the text of the notes being summarised is sent.

Nothing is sent until you ask, and only for that request. The Ask tab says which provider is answering. Your use is covered by your own agreement with that company. See Privacy.

Merging notes

Not an AI feature, but a way of thinking with your notes: pick two or more notes in the contents, right-click and choose Merge, and you get one note with a section for each. See The Contents.

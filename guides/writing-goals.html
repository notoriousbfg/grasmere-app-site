Writing Goals

Give your project, or any folder in it, a number of words to reach, by a date if you like. Grasmere then shows how far you’ve got, the words you’ve written today, and about how many a day would get you there in time.

Setting a target

  • Project ▸ Set Word Target sets a target for the whole project.
  • Right-click a folder in the contents and choose Set Word Target to set one for that folder, such as a manuscript or a chapter.

Enter the number of words and, if you want one, tick By and choose a deadline. Remove takes the target away.

Watching your progress

The bar beneath the page shows the target for the document you’re in, such as:

8,015 of 12,000 · 640 today · ~190 a day to 31 Oct

A document in a folder with its own target shows that folder’s numbers; any other document shows the project’s. Without a target, the bar shows just the words written today.

Words written today are counted from where you started this morning, and carry on if you quit and reopen the same day. Deleting words counts against them.

Where targets are kept

Targets, and the words written each day, are kept in plain Markdown in the Writing section of project.md, so you can read or edit them anywhere:

## Writing

Target: 80,000 words by 2027-03-01
- [Manuscript](manuscript/): 60,000 words by 2027-02-01

| Day | Words |
|---|---|
| 2026-10-09 | 1,240 |
| 2026-10-10 | 860 |

A row is added for each day you write. Only today’s row ever changes. The deadline in project.md can be clicked to change it, like any date.

Tasks and Due Dates

Tasks in Grasmere are ordinary Markdown checkboxes, written in any note. Grasmere gathers them in one list, and a date at the end of a task gives it a due date.

Writing tasks

- [ ] Sow green manure on the potato bed
- [x] Lift the potatoes

  • Click a box to tick or untick it. The cursor and the page stay where they are.
  • Format ▸ Tick or Untick Task (⌘↩) does the same for the line you’re on.
  • Tasks can be nested, like any list.

Due dates

Put a date at the end of a task, written either way:

- [ ] Felt the shed roof 📅 2026-10-17
- [ ] Order a trailer load due: 2026-10-14

The date is shown as a chip: today’s is highlighted, and a date that has passed on an open task is shown in a warning colour.

Click a date to pick another day from a calendar. Today, Tomorrow and Next week are a click away, the arrow keys and Return work too, and No Date takes the date off. Only the date changes; the note keeps it as plain text, with the 📅 or due: you used.

To give a task a date, right-click it and choose Set Due Date, or use Format ▸ Due Date.

Every task in one place (⇧⌘T)

Go ▸ Tasks (⇧⌘T) gathers every task in the project into the margin.

  • By note groups them under the notes they’re in. By date groups them under Overdue, Today and Upcoming.
  • Open, Done and All choose which tasks to show, and the filter box narrows them by their words.
  • Tick a task here and it’s ticked in its note. Click a due date to change it.
  • Click a task to open its note at that line, or ⌥-click to open it beside the page.

Tips

  • The 📅 form is the one the Obsidian Tasks plugin uses, so tasks you bring from Obsidian keep their dates.
  • How dates are shown in the Tasks list follows Settings ▸ General ▸ Date order. In your notes they always stay year first, so they sort the same everywhere.

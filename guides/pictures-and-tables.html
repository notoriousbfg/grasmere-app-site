Pictures and Tables

Pictures and tables are ordinary Markdown in Grasmere, drawn on the page so you see what they look like.

Pictures

A line that’s just a picture link shows the picture beneath it:

![The plot, from the gate](media/plot-plan.png)

The Markdown stays on the line above the picture, so you can still edit the caption and the address.

Adding a picture

  • Paste a screenshot or a copied image, or drop an image file onto a document. Grasmere saves it in the project’s media folder and links it where you were typing.
  • Drop a video onto a document in the same way. It’s linked like a picture, and plays on a website you build from the project.
  • Drag a picture from the contents into a document to link it.
  • Link to a picture on the web with ![](https://…). Grasmere downloads it to show it.

Working with pictures

  • Right-click a picture for Copy Image and Show in Finder.
  • Rest the pointer on a link to a picture to see a preview.
  • Rename a picture in the contents and the links to it follow, just like a document’s.

Settings

  • Settings ▸ General ▸ Save in these folders ▸ Pictures and videos chooses the folder for pasted and dropped pictures and videos (media by default, or images in a project that already has that folder).
  • Settings ▸ General ▸ Show images from the web can be turned off. Downloading a picture tells its website that you opened the document.
  • Settings ▸ Appearance ▸ Show images in the contents lists pictures alongside your documents.

Tables

Tables are drawn as tables until you click into them. Then you edit the Markdown, and when you click away, the table is drawn again.

| Crop | Sow | Harvest |
|:---|:---:|:---:|
| Garlic | October | July |
  • Format ▸ Insert Table (⌥⌘T) starts a new one.
  • Tab and ⇧Tab move between cells, and line the columns up as they go.
  • The colons in the second line set each column’s alignment: left, centre or right.

Diagrams

New in 1.3. A code block whose language is mermaid is a diagram, written as text in Mermaid’s notation, and Grasmere draws it beneath the block:

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    L[The landing] -->|8 min| F[Fold End]
    F -->|bus| S[School]
```

The text stays on the page to edit, and the drawing follows a moment after you stop typing. While a change is half-made, the last good drawing stays; if Mermaid can’t read the block, a quiet line under it says why. Click a drawing to put the cursor in its block.

Drawings take the theme’s colours and type, and go into a PDF, a Word document and a website you build (on a website, in its light and dark colours). Everything is drawn on your Mac: nothing is sent anywhere. The same block shows as a diagram on GitHub, and as code in any other Markdown editor.

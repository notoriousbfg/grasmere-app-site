Quick Capture and Sharing

Ways to get things into Grasmere while you’re busy with something else: a thought, a web page, a picture, or a passage from a conversation with an AI.

Quick capture

Click the book in the menu bar, or press ⌃⌥⌘N in any app, and a small box opens. Type a thought and press Return: it’s added to the end of today’s entry, with the time, as a paragraph of its own.

  • ⇧Return starts a new line in the box; esc closes it and keeps what you’ve typed for next time.
  • Today in … at the foot of the box chooses which project the line goes to. Grasmere remembers your choice.
  • A web address on its own becomes a link titled with the page’s name.
  • Quick capture works even when no Grasmere window is open.

In Settings ▸ Capture:

  • Show in menu bar shows or hides the book.
  • Open at login starts Grasmere quietly at login, with just the menu bar item and no windows, so capture is always ready.
  • Use a keyboard shortcut turns the shortcut on or off, and lets you record another. Use at least one of ⌘, ⌥ or ⌃.
  • Captures go to chooses the project.

Today’s entry is made if need be, in the folder and from the template set in Settings ▸ General. See Journal.

Clipping from the clipboard

Copy a web address in another app and come back to Grasmere within 20 seconds: a card offers to save it. (Something copied longer ago was for somewhere else, so you aren’t asked about it every time you come back; ⇧⌘K asks anyway.)

  • Clip to Research saves a document about the page in your clippings folder: its title, address, site and description. Clipping the same page again reuses that document.
  • Insert Link puts a link to the page, titled with its name, where you were typing.
  • A copied link to a PDF or a picture can be saved too, in the clippings folder beside a document that links to it.
  • Copied text can become a new document.

The card goes by itself after ten seconds if you don’t use it. File ▸ Clip Link from Clipboard (⇧⌘K) brings it back, and that one stays until you dismiss it. Pasting a web address into a document makes a titled link straight away.

Grasmere only downloads files of familiar kinds (PDFs, pictures, text and office documents), checks that each file is what its name says, and marks it as downloaded from the internet, as Safari does. Downloaded files are never opened by Grasmere.

Insights from AI conversations

When you’ve copied a passage from Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, the card’s ✦ menu saves it as an insight: a document in your insights folder with the passage quoted, who said it and when, and confidence: unverified in its front matter until you’ve checked it. A faint sparkle in the running head marks documents that quote a model.

The Share menu

In Safari, Finder, Photos and other apps, choose Share ▸ Grasmere to send a page, some text, a picture or a file to one of your projects, with a comment. It waits safely until Grasmere is next open, then it’s filed in the project you chose, by the same rules as clipping.

Settings

  • Settings ▸ General ▸ Offer to save what you copy turns the clipboard card off. ⇧⌘K still works.
  • Settings ▸ General ▸ Save in these folders sets the Web clippings folder (Research by default) and the Insights folder (Insights by default). They’re made when first needed.

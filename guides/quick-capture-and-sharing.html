Quick Capture and Sharing

Ways to get things into Grasmere while you’re busy with something else: a thought, a web page, a picture, or a passage from a conversation with an AI.

Quick capture

Click the book in the menu bar, or press ⌃⌥⌘N in any app, and a small box opens. Type a thought and press Return: it’s added to the end of today’s entry, with the time, as a paragraph of its own.

  • ⇧Return starts a new line in the box; esc closes it and keeps what you’ve typed for next time.
  • Today in … at the foot of the box chooses which project the line goes to. Grasmere remembers your choice.
  • A web address on its own becomes a link titled with the page’s name.
  • Quick capture works even when no Grasmere window is open.

In Settings ▸ Capture:

  • Show in menu bar shows or hides the book.
  • Open at login starts Grasmere quietly at login, with just the menu bar item and no windows, so capture is always ready.
  • Use a keyboard shortcut turns the shortcut on or off, and lets you record another. Use at least one of ⌘, ⌥ or ⌃.
  • Captures go to chooses the project.

Today’s entry is made if need be, in the folder and from the template set in Settings ▸ General. See Journal.

Documents from the clipboard

New in 1.3. Copy something in another app, then choose one of these in Grasmere’s File menu. Grasmere reads the clipboard only when you choose one; they’re dimmed when there’s nothing on it they can use.

  • New Document from Clipboard (⇧⌘K) makes a document from what you copied. Copied text becomes a document in the project’s folder, titled from its first sentence. A copied web address is saved as Save Web Page saves it: the page’s article, as a document in your research folder. A page saved before opens the document you already have.
  • Paste Link with Title puts a link to the copied web address where you’re typing, titled with the page’s name. (Pasting a web address into a document with ⌘V does the same.)
  • A copied link to a PDF or a picture is saved in the research folder, beside a document that links to it.

Grasmere only downloads files of familiar kinds (PDFs, pictures, text and office documents), checks that each file is what its name says, and marks it as downloaded from the internet, as Safari does. Downloaded files are never opened by Grasmere.

Insights from AI conversations

When you’ve copied a passage from Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, choose File ▸ New Insight from Clipboard and then where it came from. It becomes an insight: a document in your insights folder with the passage quoted, who said it and when, and confidence: unverified in its front matter until you’ve checked it. A faint sparkle in the running head marks documents that quote a model.

The Share menu

In Safari, Finder, Photos and other apps, choose Share ▸ Grasmere to send a page, some text, a picture or a file to one of your projects, with a comment. It waits safely until Grasmere is next open, then it’s filed in the project you chose. A web page is saved into that project’s research folder as Save Web Page saves it, its article as Markdown, with your comment under its title; see Research.

Settings

  • Settings ▸ General ▸ Research sets the research folder (research by default), for web pages and files from the web; Save in these folders sets the Insights folder (Insights by default). They’re made when first needed.
